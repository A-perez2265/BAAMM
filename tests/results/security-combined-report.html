🏛️ Database Row-Level Security & Concurrency Architecture

Multi-Tenant Isolation and Atomic Time-Credit Transfer Protocol

Row Level Security (RLS) Policies

Every table in the Supabase schema has RLS strictly enabled:

  • profiles: Users may only update their own profile; trigger blocks direct balance/admin mutations.
  • skills: Publicly readable, but only the author (user_id = auth.uid()) can mutate or delete.
  • exchanges: Multi-party policy restricts visibility and mutations exclusively to the participating learner and teacher.
  • notifications: Strictly isolated to the recipient (user_id = auth.uid()).
  • disputes & dispute_actions: Dispute participants have scoped access; audit actions are locked to public.is_admin().

Atomic Concurrency & Row Locking (FOR UPDATE)

The time-credit transfer procedure complete_exchange guarantees mathematical balance conservation:

  • Locking: Executes SELECT ... FOR UPDATE on both the exchange record and the learner's profile.
  • Serialization: Forces concurrent attempts to queue; subsequent threads observe status = 'completed' and abort cleanly.
  • ACID Transaction: Decrements the learner (-1 credit) and increments the teacher (+1 credit) atomically. No partial balance transfer is physically possible.
